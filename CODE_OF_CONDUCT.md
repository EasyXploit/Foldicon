# Code of conduct

Foldicon is a small project, and everyone is welcome in it, whatever their experience or background.

- Be respectful and assume good intentions.
- Keep discussions about the work, and accept that a change can be declined.
- Harassment, insults and personal attacks are not accepted, in public or in private.

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, and block anyone who ignores these rules. To report a problem, contact a
maintainer through the *Security* tab of the repository, which keeps the report private.
