# Privacy policy

Last updated: 5 November 2026.

Foldicon does not collect, store or send any personal data. It has no accounts, analytics, telemetry or ads, and it
makes no network connections.

Everything stays on your PC:

- The icon of each folder is written inside that folder, in its `desktop.ini` and, for a Fluent icon, in a hidden copy
  of the icon.
- The index of the system icons and the settings of the app are kept in `%LOCALAPPDATA%\Foldicon`.

If you report a problem on GitHub, what you write there is public and follows the privacy terms of GitHub.

Questions about this policy can be asked in the issues of the repository.
